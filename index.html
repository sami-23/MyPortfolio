The story

From flight tests to production platforms.

2018–23Quest Lab

Learning what reliability means.

I joined a UAV research lab after university and learned to fly the aircraft I was programming. Standing in a field watching my code fly changed how I approached testing: simulation tells you what you modelled; flight tells you what you missed.

I left as Team Lead, leading six engineers and mentoring five interns.

2022–Independent

Learning the business of delivery.

Freelance work became full-time contracting in January 2023. Building for clients taught me to scope honestly, explain technical trade-offs clearly, and ship when someone’s revenue depends on the date.

2023–YouGuide

Owning the system, and its direction.

I joined YouGuide as a contract developer and now lead engineering, with two engineers reporting to me. My work spans strategy, architecture and engineering standards across three products, working remotely with the Belgian team.
